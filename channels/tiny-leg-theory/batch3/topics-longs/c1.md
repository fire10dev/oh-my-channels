1. Location: L11 / mechanisms ("BATNA", "Objective criteria")
Problem: BATNA (Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement) and "Objective criteria" are tactical negotiation concepts from Fisher and Ury's business book *Getting to Yes*, not psychological or cognitive mechanisms. Furthermore, BATNA is an English business acronym that will confuse viewers and fail completely on Vietnamese TTS.
Concrete fix: Replace both with legitimate cognitive mechanisms: "Status quo bias" and "Illusion of transparency".

Concrete fix: In L24, replace "Triangulation" and "Emotional cutoff" with social psychology constructs: "Boundary permeability" and "Cognitive reappraisal". In L25, replace "Parentification" and "Scapegoating" with "Role inertia" and "Behavioral confirmation".
