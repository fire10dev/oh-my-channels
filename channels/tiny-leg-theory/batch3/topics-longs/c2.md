CRITIQUE POINTS

1. Location: L37 & L54 / mechanisms
Problem: Blatant violation of the brief's hard rule ("no two themes sharing more than 2 mechanisms"). L37 and L54 share four identical mechanisms: "Evening chronotype", "Routine cues", "Stimulus control", and "Environmental design".
Concrete fix: In L54, replace "Evening chronotype", "Routine cues", and "Environmental design" with distinct, non-overlapping mechanisms: "Intrusive thoughts", "Worry postponement", and "Pre-sleep cognitive hyperarousal". Keep only "Stimulus control".

2. Location: L54 / mechanisms
Problem: Synonymous padding. "Zeigarnik effect" and "Unfinished-task effect" are two names for the exact same empirical phenomenon (superior recall for uncompleted tasks). Listing both wastes a slot.
Concrete fix: Remove "Unfinished-task effect" and replace it with "Counterfactual rumination" or "Attention switching cost".

3. Location: L32 / mechanisms
Problem: Construct triplication. L32 lists "Self-efficacy", "Bandura’s mastery experiences", and "Self-efficacy calibration". Mastery experiences are simply one of Bandura's four sources of self-efficacy, and calibration is a minor sub-dimension.
Concrete fix: Keep "Self-efficacy". Replace "Bandura’s mastery experiences" with "Social modeling" (vicarious experience) and replace "Self-efficacy calibration" with "Action-state orientation".

4. Location: L18 / mechanisms
Problem: Workshop communication scripts and therapy frameworks listed as psychological mechanisms. "I-statements", "Nonviolent communication", "Active listening", and "Reflective listening" are therapeutic skills and interpersonal techniques, not empirical cognitive/affective mechanisms.
Concrete fix: Replace all four with genuine psychological mechanisms: "Reactive devaluation", "Fundamental attribution error", "Negative affect reciprocity", and "Perspective-taking discrepancy".

5. Location: L10 / mechanisms
Problem: Pop-business jargon. "Hyperactive hivemind phenomenon" is a book marketing term from Cal Newport, and "Asynchronous communication friction" is IT workflow jargon. Neither is a peer-reviewed psychological mechanism.
Concrete fix: Replace them with established cognitive mechanisms: "Continuous partial attention" and "Attentional residue".

6. Location: L24 & L25 / mechanisms
Problem: Violation of the "no clinical/therapy" rule. L24 lists "Differentiation of self" (the core clinical construct of Murray Bowen's family systems therapy) and L25 lists "Family homeostasis" and "Family systems theory".
Concrete fix: In L24, replace "Differentiation of self" with "Psychological reactance" or "Normative social influence". In L25, replace "Family homeostasis" and "Family systems theory" with "Behavioral inertia" and "Social role theory".

7. Location: L41 & L55 / mechanisms
Problem: Applied negotiation tactics and study aids disguised as psychology mechanisms. L41 includes "Separating people from problems" (a guideline from Fisher and Ury's negotiation book *Getting to Yes*), and L55 includes "Concept mapping" (a graphic study tool).
Concrete fix: In L41, replace with "Cognitive reappraisal" or "Confirmation bias". In L55, replace "Concept mapping" with "Levels of processing" or "Dual-process theory".

8. Location: L31 / mechanisms
Problem: Scientific inaccuracy and reliance on unreplicated theory. L31 lists "Ego depletion". The ego-depletion resource model has repeatedly failed major multi-lab preregistered replication attempts. Presenting it uncritically as fact misinforms viewers.
Concrete fix: Replace "Ego depletion" with "Threat appraisal" or "Rejection sensitivity".
