4. Location: s431 / Line 3 (text)
Problem: Scientific inaccuracy. The mechanism assigned in the brief is "Spotlight Emotions" (social self-conscious emotions like blushing and embarrassment), but the text displays "SPOTLIGHT EFFECT". In academic psychology, the Spotlight Effect (Gilovich et al.) is an egocentric cognitive bias where people overestimate how much others notice them. It is distinct from the physical threat-response of self-conscious emotions.
Concrete fix: Change text from "SPOTLIGHT EFFECT" to "EXPOSURE FEAR" (13 characters) or "SPOTLIGHT SHAME" (15 characters).
