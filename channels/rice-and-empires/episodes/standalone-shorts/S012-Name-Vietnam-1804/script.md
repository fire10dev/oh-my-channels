## Framing decision
- Avoid both simple stories: "China named Vietnam" and "the name is thousands of years old".
- What the sources support: the Nguyễn asked for **Nam Việt**; the Qing refused it (Nanyue/territory worry) and preferred **An Nam**; the Nguyễn refused An Nam;
  the two courts settled on **Việt Nam** (Qing approval 1803, investiture + announcement 1804), and each court explained the name its own way.
- Perspectives: Qing court archives (上諭檔, Jiaqing 7–8), Nguyễn chronicle (*Đại Nam thực lục*), modern historians (Trần Thị Xuân 2024; Woodside 1971 via WP; Library of Congress Country Study).
