## Framing decision
- Paradox structure (owner 23/11): a Roman emperor's face in gold from the delta mud → it isn't Roman → it leads to Funan → what the Chinese wrote vs what the mud shows → callback: the face reached us, the kingdom's own name did not.
- "Funan" is said in the first 5 s. Rome is only the clue.
- Perspectives: Chinese records (Kang Tai/Zhu Ying fragments, later dynastic histories) · archaeology (Malleret 1944; Borell 2014) · modern historian (Vickery 2003: one state or a group of ports?).
- Shared heritage: sites in Vietnam **and** Cambodia; no "really Vietnamese/Khmer".
- Banned (plan): "Roman coin/Roman gold/Romans came/Marcus Aurelius", "lost advanced civilisation", "thousands of canals", "existed only on paper".
