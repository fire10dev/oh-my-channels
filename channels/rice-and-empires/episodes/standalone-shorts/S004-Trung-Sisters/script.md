## Framing decision
- Topic is nationally sacred in Vietnam; objective angle = **record vs legend**: the earliest sources are Chinese (the enemy side), the main
  Vietnamese chronicle is ~1,400 years later. Show both versions of motive and death; do not settle what sources do not settle.
- Perspectives: Han records (*Hou Hanshu*), Vietnamese chronicle (*Toàn thư*, 1479), modern historians (Taylor 1983).
