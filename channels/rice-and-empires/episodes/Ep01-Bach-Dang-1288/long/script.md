## Why this topic (owner 2026-11-08 16:40: "2000 năm quá tham vọng… kênh không phải dạy lịch sử")
- One campaign, two puzzles: *how did a river and a tide beat the Yuan fleet — and why did the winner still send tribute?*
- Three ways of knowing the same event (review r1 §8): **military** (geography, tide, supply) → **archaeology** (what the mud can and
  cannot prove) → **diplomacy** (why victory still ended in tribute). Archaeology = what makes it distinctive; the tribute paradox = what makes it memorable.
- Demand seen 2026-11-07: Kings and Generals "How Vietnam Defended Against the Mongols" 726K; GREAT HISTORY "Why did the Mongols FAIL…" 321K.
