## Framing decision
- Owner tone: grand (ch. 0–3) → regret (ch. 5–6, through pacing and images, **no blame**) → "history has no *if*" → bright last ~45 s (ch. 7).
- Not a victim film: Champa also attacked Angkor (conventional 1177, disputed) and Đại Việt (Chế Bồng Nga, 1371). Not a victory film either:
  1471 numbers are given only as "the sources do not agree" + who says what.
- Perspectives: Cham inscriptions and 19th-c. Cham poems (C8, C9, C23), Chinese records (Song C12, Ming C18–C19), Vietnamese chronicle
  *Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư* (C13, C14, C16, C18), Khmer/modern historians (Vickery, Whitmore, Weber, Po Dharma, Ho Ping-ti, Bray).
- No "genocide/massacre" wording (Kiernan uses it; we report numbers by source instead). No modern politics.
- The video says Champa "ended as a state". It never says "the Cham disappeared".
