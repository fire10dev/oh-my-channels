## Framing decision
- Same tone as S006: objective, sourced, "historians debate". The "1,000 years" was not one continuous occupation of today's Vietnam:
  it covered the Red River region and the coast south of it, it had breaks, and the "unbroken resistance" framing is debated.
- Perspectives used: Chinese records (*Shiji*, *Hou Hanshu*, *Sanguozhi*, Tang records), Vietnamese chronicles (*Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư*, 1479),
  modern historians (Taylor, Kelley, Churchman). Every number is either cut or attributed (FACT-CHECK §4).
