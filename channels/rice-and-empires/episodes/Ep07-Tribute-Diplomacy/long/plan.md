## Angle
Vietnamese rulers fought Chinese armies again and again, yet sent tribute to the Chinese court for most of 900 years. How did both fit together?
The phrase used by historians is "nội đế ngoại vương" (emperor inside, king outside). Find who coined or used it, and attribute it.
This is the "how it worked" video. It needs few battles; diplomacy, ritual and trade carry it.

Perspectives:
- Chinese tributary records and the investiture system (Fairbank's model, and its critics);
- Vietnamese court practice;
- modern historians: Baldanza 2016 *Ming China and Vietnam*, Kang 2010 *East Asia Before the West*, Womack 2006 *China and Vietnam: The Politics of Asymmetry*, Woodside 1971, Anderson 2007.
