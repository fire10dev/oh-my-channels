# L006 plan — 1789: Quang Trung vs the Qing (long 16:9, ~9 min)

Shared rules: `docs/plans/LONG-BATCH-HANDOFF.md`.

## Angle
A peasant-born rebel general marched an army north in weeks and broke the Qing in Thăng Long over Tết. Then he asked the Qing emperor for recognition.
Both halves matter. In China, Qianlong listed the war as one of his "Ten Great Campaigns".

Perspectives:
- Qing records (Qianlong court; Fukang'an, Sun Shiyi);
- Vietnamese chronicles (*Hoàng Lê nhất thống chí*: a novelized chronicle, so attribute it);
- modern historians: Dutton 2006 *The Tây Sơn Uprising*, Taylor 2013, Woodside 1971, Waley-Cohen 2006.
