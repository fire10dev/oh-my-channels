## Angle
Four hundred and fifty years after independence, a Chinese dynasty came back and tried to make the country a province again. This time it lasted 20 years.
The video asks:
- How did it happen? (the Hồ usurpation, then the Ming pretext)
- What did the Ming try to change? (administration, books, people taken to China: research what is documented)
- How did a landowner from Lam Sơn end it?

Perspectives:
- *Ming Shilu* / *Mingshi* (Ming side);
- *Toàn thư*, and Nguyễn Trãi's *Bình Ngô đại cáo* (Vietnamese side, a victory proclamation, so attribute it);
- modern historians: Whitmore 1985 *Vietnam, Hồ Quý Ly, and the Ming*, Taylor 2013, Kiernan 2019, Sun Laichen on firearms.
