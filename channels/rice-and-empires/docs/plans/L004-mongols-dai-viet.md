## Angle
The empire that took China, and Baghdad before it, captured Thăng Long three times and still could not hold Đại Việt. Why?
The video's answer: strategy (abandon the capital, cut supply, the river), climate and disease, and politics in the Yuan court.
It is not "one heroic victory".

Perspectives to use:
- the *Yuanshi* (Yuan side);
- *Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư* (Trần side);
- *An Nam chí lược* (Lê Tắc, a Trần official who defected to the Yuan; a third view);
- modern historians: Baldanza 2016, Lo 2012, Taylor 2013, Anderson 2014, Haw 2013.

## Sensitive points
- Army sizes: never give one figure. Vietnamese chronicles say 500,000; modern estimates are much lower (S002 C7). Say that.
- "Defeated the Mongols": the Yuan withdrew, but Đại Việt kept sending tribute. Say both.
