## Framing decision
- Not "why the Mongols failed": 1258 ended with Trần submission/tribute, and Uriyangkhadai left mainly to attack Song (C4).
- Defensible: **took the capital three times (1258, 1285, 1288) but never held Đại Việt**; ending is nuanced: Đại Việt won, then still sent tribute (C15).
- Two perspectives: Yuan view (vassal refusing to come to court) vs Trần view (paper submission yes, kingdom no) — C14.
