## Framing decision (from research, not from memory)
- Original idea "Why the Mongols failed 3 times" is **not objective**: 1258 did not end in a clear Mongol defeat — Thăng Long fell, the Trần
  later sent tribute, and Uriyangkhadai left in 1259 mainly for strategy (Song campaign), heat/disease is contested (C4).
- Defensible framing: **captured the capital three times (1258, 1285, 1288) but never held Đại Việt**; and the ending is nuanced: Đại Việt still
  paid tribute after winning (C15).
