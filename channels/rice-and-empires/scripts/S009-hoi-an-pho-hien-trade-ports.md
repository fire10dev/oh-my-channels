## Framing decision
- Counter the image of a closed, purely agrarian Vietnam: in the 1600s–1700s both rival regimes (Nguyễn in the south, Trịnh–Lê in the north)
  ran international ports. Show the foreign side (Japanese, Chinese, Dutch records) and the Vietnamese side. Keep numbers out.
