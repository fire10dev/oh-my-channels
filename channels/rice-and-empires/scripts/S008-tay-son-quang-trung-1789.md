## Framing decision
- Avoid the textbook "200,000 Qing troops crushed" framing: troop numbers vary from ~10,000 to 500,000 by source → say so.
- Show both sides: Vietnamese historians call it one of the greatest victories; the Qianlong emperor counted the Annam campaign among his
  "Ten Great Campaigns". And the twist: after winning, Quang Trung sought Qing recognition — and (per sources) sent a stand-in to Beijing.
