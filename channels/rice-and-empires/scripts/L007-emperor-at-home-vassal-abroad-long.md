## Framing decision
- "How it worked" video: diplomacy, ritual, paper titles. Few battles, no troop numbers except where attributed.
- Tribute ≠ being ruled. Both readings are given: a Chinese-centred hierarchy (Fairbank model; Kang 2010) and a flexible practice /
  diplomatic fiction (Perdue 2009 and Kang's critics). Womack's "asymmetry" is attributed, and the modern section stays historical (ends 1885).
- Perspectives: Chinese court records (*Songshi*, Ming and Qing records via Baldanza, Chan, Tsai, Dardess), Vietnamese chronicles
  (*Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư*), Korean records (Yi Su-gwang, *Bugwollok*), modern historians (Taylor, Woodside, Kelley, Kang, Womack, Baldanza, Perdue).
- The phrase *nội đế ngoại vương* ("emperor inside, king outside"): we found **no reliable source for who coined it**. Narration says
  "historians sometimes sum it up as…", no coiner named.
