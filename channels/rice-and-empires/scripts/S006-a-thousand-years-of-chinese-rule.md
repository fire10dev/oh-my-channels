## Framing decision
- Sensitive, nationalist-heavy topic. Objective angle: the "1,000 years" was **not one continuous occupation of today's Vietnam**:
  it covered the Red River region, had breaks, and modern historians debate the "unbroken resistance" narrative.
- Perspectives: Chinese administrative view (province/commandery), Vietnamese national historiography, modern scholarship (Taylor, Kelley, Churchman).
