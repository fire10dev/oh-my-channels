# S007 — Quốc ngữ: how Vietnam ended up writing in the Latin alphabet (Short 9:16, ~85 s)

## Framing decision
- Popular myth: "Alexandre de Rhodes invented Quốc ngữ". Record: a team effort of Portuguese/Italian Jesuits, Rhodes published the key dictionary (1651);
  the script spread only centuries later through colonial policy **and** Vietnamese reformers/state literacy campaigns.
- Perspectives: missionaries (purpose: evangelization), French colonial state (cut ties to Chinese learning — Pears), Vietnamese reformers.
