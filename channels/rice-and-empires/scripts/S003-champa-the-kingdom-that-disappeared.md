# S003 — Champa: the kingdom that vanished from the map (Short 9:16, ~100 s)

Status: **v1 — research round 1 + Codex review pending — owner review of VI column pending.** Style: **antique map / parchment**.
Changelog: v1 2026-11-08 00:10 first draft.

## Framing decision
- Not "a lost civilization destroyed by Vietnam": Champa was a long-lived, plural polity that also raided Đại Việt (C7); it ended as a state
  in stages (1471, 1697, 1832), and the Cham people still live in Vietnam and Cambodia (C10).
- Perspectives: Chinese records (C2), Vietnamese conquest (C8–C9), a Cham voice ("Annam destroyed our country", C8), modern historians' debate (C6).
