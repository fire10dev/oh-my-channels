## Framing decision
- Not "one heroic victory". The Mongols/Yuan took Thăng Long three times (1258, 1285, 1288) and never held Đại Việt.
  1258 ended in a tribute deal, not a defeat (C4, C5); 1288 ended in a Yuan withdrawal *and* renewed Trần tribute (C15).
- Answer to "why": strategy (give up the capital, keep the army), supply (C11), climate/disease (C4, C27), Yuan priorities (C16, C29).
- Perspectives: *Yuanshi* 元史 ch. 209 (Yuan side, read directly) · *Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư* (Trần side, ch. 5 read directly) ·
  Lê Tắc's *An Nam chí lược* (a defector's view, C26) · modern historians (Baldanza, Taylor, Lo, Anderson, Haw).
- Numbers: army sizes only as ranges with attribution (C7, C19).
