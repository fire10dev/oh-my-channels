## Framing decision
- Popular story "Siam sacked Angkor in 1431 and it was abandoned" is too simple. Present it as **competing explanations**, with the newest
  evidence (sediment cores, 2019) pointing to a slow decline, and stress that Angkor was never fully emptied (Angkor Wat stayed a Buddhist site).
- Perspectives: chronicle-based war story (Siamese/Cambodian chronicles), climate scientists (tree rings), archaeologists (sediment, lidar).
