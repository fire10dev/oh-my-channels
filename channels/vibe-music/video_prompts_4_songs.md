## 1. Matcha Cloud II — 80 BPM

Nhạc: Cozy ambient chillout, soothing felt piano, soft synth pads, continuous calm melody, relaxing study focus music, 80 bpm
Bìa: tách matcha bốc khói cạnh đàn piano gỗ, chồng sách, cửa sổ nắng nhẹ.

```
Create a relaxing 10-second seamless-loop animation for a YouTube AI music channel called VIBE MUSIC AI MAKER.

SCENE: A cozy kawaii-inspired music corner on a calm late afternoon. A cute character wearing oversized headphones sits at an old upright wooden piano, eyes softly closed, resting one hand on the keys. On the piano ledge: a handmade ceramic cup of frothy matcha latte with a gentle wisp of steam, a small stack of books, an open notebook and a pencil. A window beside the piano shows soft clouds and warm diffused light; small potted plants sit on the sill.

COLOR PALETTE: Matcha green, creamy beige, warm walnut wood, soft peach and gentle golden light. Soft gradients, delicate shadows, subtle ambient glow. No neon, no high contrast.

MOTION: Extremely subtle, slow movements that suit a gentle 80 BPM felt piano track. Steam curls slowly from the matcha cup. Clouds drift almost imperceptibly outside the window. Plant leaves sway very slightly. The character breathes gently. Dust motes float slowly in the sunbeam. All movement is calm, delicate and continuous.

MUSIC VISUALIZATION: Very subtle, smooth flowing audio waves near the bottom edge, softly glowing in matcha green and cream, suggesting a continuous felt piano melody and soft synth pads. Minimal and unobtrusive, no spikes or pulses.

CAMERA: Completely static. No zoom, pan, tilt, shake or cuts.

LOOP REQUIREMENTS: Perfectly seamless loop for an extended YouTube study video. Keep character, piano, furniture, lighting and composition identical throughout. All animated elements move in smooth cyclic paths: the steam loops naturally, clouds and dust motes follow repeating paths, leaves return to their starting position. The final frame must match the first frame as closely as possible, with no jump, flicker or transition.

ART STYLE: Premium cozy kawaii digital illustration, soft anime-inspired aesthetics, warm painterly textures, delicate details, warm cinematic lighting, subtle bloom, clean composition, adorable but not childish.

MOOD AND PURPOSE: Calm, warm, comforting and focused. For study, focus, reading and quiet relaxation. Visual rhythm is slow and unobtrusive, complementing an 80 BPM melody without pulsing to every beat.

QUALITY: High-quality rendering, consistent character design, stable geometry, crisp details, no distortions.

RESTRICTIONS: No dialogue, no subtitles, no text, no logos, no watermarks, no sudden movements, no flashing lights, no fast animation, no camera movement, no character deformation.
```
